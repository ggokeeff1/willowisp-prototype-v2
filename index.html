Why this matters
The EPA won't accept ammonia readings unless your sensors have been checked against a certified reference instrument. Every 6 months, a Teagasc technician visits and cross-checks your sensors against a calibrated FTIR analyser. Once they pass, your continuous readings count as official data — no separate annual survey needed, and the data goes straight into your annual report.
